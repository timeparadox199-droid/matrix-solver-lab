# 🧮 Linearis — The Equation Forge

> *Where numbers stop being a chore and start being a conversation.*

A tactile, browser-first training ground for anyone who wants to get genuinely good at solving linear equations — not by memorizing tricks, but by building an intuition that survives contact with real problems.

Rendered for humans, tuned for curiosity, and designed to be picked up in under a minute.

---

## 🌟 Why Linearis Exists

Most equation trainers feel like flashcard drills from a decade ago. You type an answer, you get a red X, you move on — and nothing actually sticks. Linearis takes a different stance.

Think of it like a **gym for algebra**. Every session adjusts to your current strength, adds a little resistance at the right moment, and never lets you plateau silently. The result: a habit of solving that feels almost meditative rather than punitive.

Whether you're a student brushing up before an exam, a teacher looking for a warm-up activity, a parent supporting a learner at home, or a lifelong tinkerer who simply enjoys the elegance of `ax + b = c` — Linearis meets you where you are.

---

## 🚀 Feature Highlights

**Adaptive difficulty engine**  
Problems shift in complexity based on your rolling accuracy, response time, and self-reported confidence. No two sessions look the same.

**Step-by-step reasoning mode**  
Rather than just checking the final answer, Linearis can walk through each transformation — isolating variables, balancing sides, and verifying the result — so the *method* becomes second nature.

**Responsive interface that respects your screen**  
From a phone on the bus to an ultrawide monitor at a desk, the layout reflows gracefully. No pinching, no zooming, no horizontal scrollbars of shame.

**Multilingual by design**  
Prompt text, hints, and UI labels ship in a growing set of languages. Math is universal; the wrapper around it shouldn't be a barrier.

**Round-the-clock assistance**  
A built-in help channel is available at every hour of every day, every day of the year — because late-night study sessions don't schedule themselves around business hours.

**Progress memory**  
Your streak, mastered patterns, and areas that need polish are stored locally so you can pick up exactly where you left off, on any session.

**Zero-friction onboarding**  
Open the page. Solve an equation. That's it. No accounts required to start learning.

**Open and transparent**  
Source, roadmap, and issue tracker are all public. Every improvement is a pull request away.

## 🧠 The Design Philosophy

Linearis is built on three quiet convictions:

1. **Fluency beats memorization.** Knowing *why* you move a term across the equals sign is worth ten memorized shortcuts.
2. **Feedback should teach, not judge.** Every wrong answer is an opportunity to reveal the next insight.
3. **Practice should feel like play.** A little delight in the interface goes a long way toward a durable habit.

The interface borrows from the aesthetics of a well-worn notebook — clean typography, generous whitespace, subtle motion — rather than the frantic energy of a quiz show.

## 🛠️ Built With Intention

Linearis favors a lean, dependency-light stack so it loads quickly on modest hardware and aging devices. The architecture separates the **problem generator**, the **difficulty controller**, and the **presentation layer**, which means each piece can evolve independently without destabilizing the others.

Contributors will find a strongly typed codebase, clear module boundaries, and a test suite that prioritizes behavioral correctness over coverage vanity metrics.

## 🌍 Accessibility Commitment

Mathematics education has historically excluded people. Linearis actively works against that pattern:

- Keyboard-navigable controls throughout
- Sufficient color contrast in every theme
- Screen-reader-friendly labeling of interactive elements
- Motion that respects the user's reduced-motion preference

If you encounter a barrier, please report it — accessibility issues are treated as bugs, not feature requests.
